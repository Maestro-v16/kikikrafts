Our cloth

What is adire?

Adire is a Yoruba resist-dyed cloth. Parts of the fabric are tied, stitched or covered so the dye cannot reach them. When the cloth is opened, the pattern appears.

At KikiKrafts, we bring this tradition into everyday life. We make adire you can wear to class, to work and to celebrate, so heritage goes with you.

Browse the products

  1. 1. Design the pattern

    Choose the marks, rings or stripes, then tie, stitch or cover the cloth.

  2. 2. Resist and dye

    The covered parts resist the dye. The open parts take the colour.

  3. 3. Dry and finish

    Let it dry, open it up and see the pattern come alive.
